Adversarial auditing of K-NAF privacy

IIT Madras · Privacy in AI course project

  • Built an auditing pipeline that attacks K-NAF decoding guarantees with targeted prompts, across optimization, validation, stress tests, and held-out evaluation, over several global K budgets and model variants.
  • Measured certified privacy risk with UEBB, effective budget, and ρ. Both settings stayed certified in aggregate.
  • At k = 3 the audit transferred better to held-out data, separated attacks more sharply, and stayed more reliable than at k = 5.
